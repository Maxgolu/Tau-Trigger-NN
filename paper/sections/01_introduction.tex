\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ============================================================================
The ATLAS Level-1 trigger must distinguish narrow tau deposits from ordinary
jets while collisions arrive at \SI{40}{MHz}, retaining a small fraction for
reconstruction~\cite{atlas_trigger_run3,atlas_phase1_tdr}. We study compact
neural networks for calorimeter Trigger Objects (TOBs) supplied by the electron
Feature Extractor (eFEX), targeting a $0.5\%$ event fake rate. Single-TOB
models test features, architectures and objectives; the pair extension combines
two TOBs before the event decision. Their efficiencies use different denominators:
truth-$\tau$ objects and signal events, respectively
(\cref{sec:input_unit,sec:pair_results,app:pair_protocol}).
